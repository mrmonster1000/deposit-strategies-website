RATE DATA

We track deposit rates from 21 UK banks daily, covering over 1,000 individual products. The data reveals four distinct product categories, each with different competitive dynamics.

CATEGORY RATE RANGE KEY CHARACTERISTICS
Regular Savers (Dated) 5.0-7.5% Fixed 6-12 month terms, monthly caps, headline rates
Regular Savers (Undated) 5.5-7.1% No maturity, withdrawal penalties, rate flexibility
Bonus Savings 4.0-5.0% 12-month bonus period, retention risk, rate drop-off
Instant Access 1.1-4.6% Full liquidity, widest rate dispersion, digital vs traditional

Data as at January 2026. Rates subject to change.

ABOUT

DepositStrategies is the sister publication to IRRBB.com, focused on the business side of deposit pricing.

While IRRBB.com serves ALM teams with technical analysis of interest rate risk measurement and modelling, DepositStrategies is built for the teams who actually set rates: product managers, pricing analysts, and commercial strategists.

We believe better market intelligence leads to better pricing decisions. Our goal is to give deposit pricing teams the same depth of competitive insight that trading desks take for granted.
