ABOUT

Game of Radical Abundance is a satirical strategy game about the AI development race toward post-scarcity abundance.

Navigate the speed-vs-safety tradeoff as real-world AI leaders. Every decision teaches real AI governance concepts through memorable, character-driven scenarios.

Inspired by the classic LucasArts adventures of the 1990s.

Design: The Radical Abundance Team
Engine: HTML5 Canvas + Vanilla JS
